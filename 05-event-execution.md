# 5. Event Execution (D-Day)

## Suggested Agenda

### Standard Full-Day Format (~6 hours)

| Time | Session | Duration | Notes |
|------|---------|----------|-------|
| 09:00–09:10 | **Introduction** | 10 min | Welcome, agenda overview, logistics |
| 09:10–09:30 | **Frontier Firm Vision** | 20 min | AI transformation context, "why agents" |
| 09:30–10:30 | **Copilot Chat in Action** | 60 min | Live demos, Q&A — customers learn from each other |
| 10:30–10:45 | **Break** | 15 min | |
| 10:45–11:45 | **Copilot and Agents for Work** | 60 min | Agent capabilities, Copilot Studio, what's possible |
| 11:45–12:30 | **Hackathon Brief and Ideation** | 45 min | Rules, team formation, use case exploration at tables |
| 12:30–13:00 | **Lunch** | 30 min | |
| 13:00–14:00 | **Build Your Agent** | 60 min | Hands-on hackathon at tables |
| 14:00–14:30 | **Showcase / Pitch Your Agent** | 30 min | 5 min per table |
| 14:30–14:45 | **Winners & Awards** | 15 min | Jury scores, prizes |
| 14:45–15:00 | **Next Steps, Feedback, Closing** | 15 min | Opportunities, feedback form, CTA |

| 00:45–01:00 | Find & Meet Your Table | 15 min |

## Morning Session: Learning & Inspiration

### Presentation Content

Include in the morning presentations:
- **AI transformation context** — why agents matter now
- **ROI messaging** — include licensing context, business value
- **Live demonstrations** — Copilot Chat, Agent capabilities
- **Partner service offerings** — what happens after today

> ⚠️ **Field Insight:** Make sure there's an ROI message INCLUDING licensing in the presentations. Don't just demo the tech.

### The Call to Action

> The Call to Action is critical and should be introduced during the presentation time — NOT left as an afterthought at the end of the day. Frame the event as a first step in a journey.

## Afternoon Session: Hands-On Hackathon

### Table Setup

| Element | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| **People per table** | 4–5 customer attendees + 1 technical expert coach + 1 functional expert |
| **Organization** | One table per industry/function |
| **Materials** | Printed login instructions, scenario brief, evaluation template |
| **Environment** | One dedicated Power Platform environment per table |

### Flow at Each Table

1. **Review the example use case** (15 min) — pre-built scenario to spark thinking
2. **Ideate their own use case** (15 min) — building on or departing from the example
3. **Build the agent** (60+ min) — partner technical expert handles configuration; customer focuses on prompting, testing, refining
4. **Prepare pitch** (15 min) — 5-minute presentation to jury

### Reducing Complexity

> ⚠️ **Field Insight:** Reduce as much complexity as possible BEFORE the event. Partner technical person pre-configures tricky parts; customer gets the easy, visible parts (prompting, testing, refining). Give customers the satisfying work, not the frustrating work.

### Using Copilot M365 During the Event

Use Copilot for Microsoft 365 to help set up presentations, generate content, and assist teams — this also demonstrates its value in real-time.

## Success Ingredients

1. **Hands-on Practice**: Immediately apply learning to hypothetical scenarios. Live Q&A after each segment enables customers to learn from each other.

2. **Relevance to Attendees**: Scenarios must be similar enough to attendees' business needs. Dummy tenants offer a range of industry and role functions.

3. **Multi-disciplinary Teams**: Mix of business decision-makers and process users fosters comprehensive understanding.

4. **Peer Learning**: Customers benefit from learning from each other.
   - **Similar learning stages**: Choose customers in the same stage (Acquire/Monetize/Grow)
   - **Hypothetical yet relevant**: Different customers collaborating on a hypothetical use case leads to more open sharing
   - **Collaborative session design**: Leave time for free discussion at tables

5. **Fun & Competition**: Healthy competition between tables keeps energy high.
   - Small prizes for winning tables
   - X-Factor-style judging where tables pitch their agents
   - Keep energy going throughout the afternoon

> 🔍 **[Enrichment]** From multiple event recaps: **Pre-defined scenarios accelerate productivity** much more than open ideation alone. Hands-on practice with expert support is consistently rated as the highest-value component by attendees. **Time-boxed formats (1 day) scale better across regions** than multi-day events.

## Award Criteria

### Suggested Scoring Dimensions

| Criteria | What to Evaluate |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Agent Excellence** | How well does the agent use available technology? |
| **Innovation** | How creative and original is the solution? |
| **Feasibility** | How practical is it? Can it be realistically implemented? |
| **Impact** | How much potential to improve user experience? Does it solve a real problem? |
| **Presentation** | Clear, concise, engaging communication of the solution and its benefits? |

### Pitch Format
- **5 minutes per table** to present their agent
- Jury comprised of representatives from Microsoft, Partner, and optionally Customer

### Jury Composition
- At least one Microsoft representative (PM, GBB, or expert)
- At least one Partner representative (senior/executive)
- Optional: Customer executive or industry expert

## Logistics & Cost

### Cost Items to Plan

| Item | Notes |
|------|-------|
| **Venue** | Partner or Microsoft office preferred; external venue if needed |
| **Food** | Breakfast, lunch, snacks — cost per head |
| **Competition prizes** | Small prizes for 1st, 2nd, 3rd |
| **Hostess/reception** | Usually required at Microsoft offices |
| **Name badges** | |
| **Banners/branding** | May only be needed for external venues |
| **Notepads, pens, water** | Basic supplies |
| **Spektra environments** | ~$1,000 USD per event (up to 50 participants); PO required |
| **Merchandise** | At partner's discretion — cannot be covered by Microsoft funding |

### What Microsoft Can Fund
- PSA support (via PMX request)
- TPM budget for follow-up O.O. workshops (when it accelerates opportunity creation)
- Speaker time and expertise

### What Partners Cover
- Venue, food, logistics, materials
- Spektra environment costs (PO required)
- Merchandise and prizes

## Capturing Outputs

During the event, capture:
- **Team outputs and pitch decks** → feeds into Book of Agents
- **Photos and testimonials** → marketing and future event promotion
- **Feedback forms** → event improvement
- **Customer interest levels** → lead qualification

> Compile the Book of Agents WHILE judges deliberate. Use team pitch decks as input. See [Follow-Up & Conversion](06-follow-up.md#book-of-agents) for Book of Agents details.
