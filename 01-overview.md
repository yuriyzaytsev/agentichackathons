# 1. Overview & Positioning

## What Is an Agent Hackathon?

A partner-led, Microsoft-supported event (typically one day) that **energizes and trains customers to ideate Agent use cases** and design Agent solutions using Microsoft 365 Copilot and Copilot Studio.

The event is structured around **hands-on scenario building** — customers work in teams at tables (organized by industry or business function) to envision, prototype, and pitch AI agents addressing real business challenges.

### Key Principle

> ⚠️ **Field Insight:** The goal is NOT to complete the customer's actual scenario. The goal is to **INSPIRE** that their scenario CAN be done. Use invented companies and simplified scenarios to show what's possible.

## Objectives

1. **Energize and train** customers to ideate Agent use cases
2. **Design Agent solutions** by industry and/or role function aligned with "AI Transformation" strategy
3. **Generate qualified leads** for partner services and Microsoft products
4. **Create a "Book of Agents"** — documented use cases customers can take away
5. **Qualify next steps** — MCI nominations, Copilot Accelerate engagements, partner offerings

## Key Outcomes

| Outcome | Owner |
|---------|-------|
| Documentation of Agent use cases and solutions (Book of Agents) | Partner + Microsoft |
| Customer nominations for MCI (Copilot + Power Accelerate) | Microsoft (with partner input) |
| Agent opportunities raised via cosell process | Partner |
| Qualified leads with clear next steps | Partner + Microsoft AE |

## Position in Sales Process

The Agent Hackathon sits at the **Listen & Consult → Inspire & Design** stage boundary:

```
Partnership          → Demand Generation → Agent Hackathon → Qualify → Accelerate → Transform
(Joint value prop)     (Invite customers)   (MQLs to SQLs)    (Assess)  (O.O./MCI)   (Deploy)
```

### Sales Stage Mapping

| Stage | Activity | Hackathon Role |
|-------|----------|----------------|
| **Stage 1: Listen & Consult** | Customers come with ideas, leave with validated use cases | One-to-Many Agent Hackathon |
| **Stage 2: Inspire & Design** | Assess applicability to customer's organization | One-to-One follow-up engagement |
| **Qualify (MQLs → SQLs)** | Identify, qualify, structure Copilot opportunity | Book of Agents → Partner services |
| **Accelerate** | Guide toward validated scenario, ready-to-start project | Copilot Accelerate / MCI |

> 🔍 **[Enrichment]** Agent hackathons are explicitly positioned as **lead-generation + opportunity-shaping** motions, NOT skilling events. Success = qualified next conversation booked + lead created + AE looped in.

## Event Formats

### Primary: One-Day Agent Hackathon (1:Many)
- **Duration**: Full day (typically 9am–3pm or 4 hours effective hands-on)
- **Audience**: Multiple customers from multiple organizations
- **Tables**: 4–6 attendees per table, organized by industry or function
- **Best for**: Lead generation, broad demand, partner visibility

### Primary: One-Day Agent Hackathon (1:Few / 1:1)
- **Duration**: Full day
- **Audience**: Single customer or small group from same organization
- **Best for**: Deeper engagement with hot leads, can use customer environment
- **Note**: Can be part of a Copilot + Power Accelerate engagement

### Alternative: AI Challenge (Multi-Week Format)

> ⚠️ **Field Insight:** An alternative format framed as a "challenge" rather than single-day hackathon. Teams are built across Microsoft, partner, and customer — creating bonding and trust. Runs over 3–4 weeks with event character. More aligned to making customer scenarios work with objective of MVP.

Key differences from single-day hackathon:
- Initial kickoff (2 days) → work done between events → celebration/showcase
- Teams want to shine at the finale — drives higher engagement
- Use cases can be more complex since there's time between events
- Objective is MVP, not just inspiration
- Better suited when lead is already hot and needs deeper qualification

## Who Should Run an Agent Hackathon

| Customer Size | Recommended Format |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Medium to Large | 1:Few customers or single customer |
| Smaller customers | 1:Many or 1:Few |
| Enterprise (hot lead) | 1:1 with customer environment |

## Success Definition

> - **Event success** = qualified next conversation booked
> - **GTM success** = lead created + seller looped in
> - **Strategic success** = repeatable scenario enters partner offering
